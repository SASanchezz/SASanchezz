#### What I do

- **Build backends from scratch.** Most recently a real-money betting platform, end to end. Before that, a custom e-commerce backend (Pixory) that replaced an expensive third-party platform and cut ongoing operating costs.
- **Serve the frontend teams.** As primary backend engineer I design the APIs and data model for up to 5 frontend engineers per project; across 3 projects that came to about 10 engineers in my first 6 months.
- **Lead engineers.** As a team lead I rebuilt the code review process and brought review time down from 4 days to under 24 hours. I agree API contracts with design and frontend before development starts, so less work gets redone.
- **Own delivery.** I moved a team from environment-named branches to trunk-based development with immutable artifact promotion (`vX.Y.Z-dev.<sha>` → clean SemVer releases), with CI/CD on Jenkins.
- **Bring AI into engineering.** I introduced Claude Code to C-level leadership and rolled it out to the dev team: prompting training, plus shared skills and rules.
- **Think in business terms.** With a Master's in Business & Financial Economics, I advise founders on schemas, on scope, and on what to build first, so the team doesn't over-engineer early or spend budget on cloud services it doesn't need.
